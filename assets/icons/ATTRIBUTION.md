# Icon attribution

The nine official Adobado marks (launcher plus eight applications) are original artwork by @smouj, the project owner. The owner supplied `Adobado_Icon_Pack_Final.zip` and authorized redistribution under MIT OR Apache-2.0. Its `SHA256SUMS.txt` was verified before synchronization.

- `launcher.png` / `launcher.ico` and matching copies in `launcher/`: Adobado Kit launcher.
- `{photo,art,film,effect,light,sound,design,pdf}.{png,ico}`: the eight product marks.
- `svg/*.svg`: packaging wrappers embedding the corresponding official 512 px PNG.
- `family.png`: generated contact sheet of the eight app PNGs.
- `assets/social-preview.png`: generated from a real FilmAdobado Windows screenshot with the official launcher and app marks.
